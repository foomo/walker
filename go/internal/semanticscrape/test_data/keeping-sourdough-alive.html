Keeping a sourdough starter alive

Feed it, watch it, and don’t panic.

October 21, 2026, by Lena Huber

Feeding

Discard half, then add equal weights of flour and water. Do this once a day at room temperature.

Signs it’s healthy

  • It doubles within 4–8 hours.
  • It smells tangy, not sharp.
  • The surface is full of bubbles.

Storing it

In the fridge, a starter needs feeding only once a week. Let it warm up for a few hours before baking.
