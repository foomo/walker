Welcome to the semantic shape demo shop

Funny robots and unusual cars - and a small website that shows how an SEO specification becomes an executable shape. Every page group of this shop is described by a semantic shape file, and walker validates and scores every page against it.

How this demo works

Every page sends its group in the x-next-js-app response header: index, category or product. The shapes for these groups live in go/internal/demoshop/shapes, together with components for the category and product tiles and the feature sections of a product.

Most products are in great shape, a few have deliberate SEO flaws, so you can see findings, penalties and lower scores: Tiny Tim, Lazybot Deluxe, the station wagon with the very long name and the Crypto Cruiser.
